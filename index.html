Inside the 30-day roadmap

Every roadmap follows the same four-week shape, filled in with the steps for your model.

Week 1

Set up

Pick your exact offer, open the accounts you need, and put the basic pieces in place. Nothing fancy — just standing at the start line, ready.

Week 2

Build

Make the thing: the service page, the product, the first pieces of content. Small, finished and real beats big, perfect and imaginary.

Week 3

Week 4

The six paths the compass knows

Every recommendation comes from one of these models. None of them is “the best” — the best one is the one that fits your life.

01

Freelancing & services

Sell a skill you already have — editing, design, writing, admin — to clients who pay this month, not someday. The fastest route from zero to a first dollar.

Best ifYou have a usable skill and need income soon. Watch outYour hours are the ceiling — plan an exit into products later.
02

Digital products & printables

Make something once — a planner, a game pack, a template, a guide — and sell the file forever. No stock, no shipping, no clients.

Best ifYou like making things and have a small budget to start. Watch outSales are slow until your listings and pins start compounding.
03

Content & audience

Videos, posts and series that teach or entertain a niche — then earn from ads, sponsors and your own products once the audience trusts you.

Best ifYou can show up consistently and play a longer game. Watch outGrowth is quiet at first; most people quit in the quiet.
04

Print on demand

Your designs on shirts, mugs and prints that a print partner makes and ships only when someone orders. You never touch inventory.

Best ifYou have design ideas and zero storage space or stock money. Watch outMargins per item are thin — designs and niches do the heavy lifting.
05

Affiliate content

Honest reviews, comparisons and resource pages that recommend tools you genuinely use, earning a commission when readers buy through your links.

Best ifYou write clearly and already have — or can build — some traffic. Watch outIt needs an audience first; it’s a second engine, not a first one.
06

Micro-tools & software

A small tool that solves one annoying problem — a calculator, a generator, a checker — sold as a subscription or one-off. The highest ceiling, the slowest start.

Best ifYou can build, or partner with someone who can, and can wait. Watch outDon’t start here with no audience and no revenue — graduate into it.

Straight answers

Is this another “get rich quick” thing?

No — and be suspicious of anything that is. Income Compass gives you a direction and a 30-day plan; the income still comes from the work you put in, and the tracker exists precisely so you can see your real numbers instead of anyone’s promises.

I have a job already. Is this for me?

Yes — the assessment is built around your real life. It asks how many hours you can honestly give each week, and matches you to models that fit around a job instead of demanding you quit one.

I have no skills and no budget. Can it still help?

That’s exactly the profile it was built for. Some models — printables, print on demand, content — can be started with little or no money, and the assessment weighs your starting budget heavily so it never points you at a path you can’t afford to begin.

What do I actually get with Pro?

The full income assessment, your top three income models scored against your profile, a personal 30-day roadmap for your best fit, the income tracker, the opportunity explorer, and the complete 10-layer Income-Transformation playbook.
